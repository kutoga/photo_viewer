MAKE YOURSELF AT HOME

A small guide to your world.

Start with a folder

Add as many folders as you like. Subfolders are included. Only photos and videos with embedded GPS coordinates appear on the map. Your original files are never edited or moved.

See the big picture. Then get closer.

Auto switches from heatmap to photo clusters as you zoom. Choose Heatmap or Bubbles to keep that display at every zoom level. Click a bubble to explore its memories. “Browse this area” shows photos inside the current map boundaries. Hide the sidebar with the button beside the page title to give the map more room.

Find a moment

Search a filename, choose a source folder, or narrow the timeline. Undated photos stay visible. “Use view dates” applies the earliest and latest dates of photos in the current map area, open area panel, or gallery results. Use “Use these dates” in an area panel to focus on that selection. Reset the date range to see all dates again. Switch to Gallery for a larger view of every matching memory.

Keep your library up to date

Use the rescan button beside Folders after adding or changing files. Unchanged files are skipped. Removing a folder only removes its cached copies. Internet is needed for map tiles, not for your photo library.
